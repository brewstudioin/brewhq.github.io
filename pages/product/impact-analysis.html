See change implications or the ‘blast radius’

Brew maps change across Data / Server / Client / UI/UX so you catch risks early, scope accurately and estimate well.

What it does

  • Full‑stack perspective — One view across data models, services, clients, and UI.

  • Forward & backward impact — Track downstream effects and upstream dependencies.

  • Related requirements — Surface adjacent work to consolidate or de‑duplicate.

  • Risk flags — Identify migrations, breaking contracts, and cross‑team handoffs.

  • Decision trails — Notes and assumptions captured alongside the analysis.

How it helps

  • Impact matrix with editable notes.

  • Change type tags per element.

  • Complexity & risk scoring to inform estimates.

  • Code adjacency hints (from GitHub) for likely modules/files.

  • Export impact summary into Jira or a linked doc.
