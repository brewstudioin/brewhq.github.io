Development ready plans in minutes

From requirement to a clear, executable checklist—tasks, dependencies, success criteria, estimates, and acceptance criteria included.

What it does

  • Stepwise plan — Ordered tasks with dependencies and owners.

  • Acceptance criteria — Concrete “done” definitions prevent rework.

  • Estimates that stick — Context‑weighted sizing from impact signals.

  • Test ideas & risks — Bake quality into the plan from day one.

  • Sync to Jira — Create or update issues/epics with full context.

How it helps

  • Plan generator seeded by impact matrix + your conventions.

  • Task anatomy: title, description, dependencies, ACs, estimate, risk notes.

  • Plan views: by stream (Data/Server/Client/UI) or by sequence.

  • Velocity preview: roll‑ups for capacity and forecast.

  • Exports: Jira issues, markdown, or shareable link.

FAQs

  • Can we enforce our Acceptance criterias? — Yes, customize task templates and checklists.

  • How do estimates work? — Derived from complexity/risk tags; you can edit, sync to JIRA.

  • Do agents pull plans directly? — Share links with teammates or copy/paste.
