Clarity from day one

Turn messy requirements into impact maps and implementation plans that help stakeholders stay aligned.

Pain & Promise

  • Pain: Vague specs, shifting scope, endless reviews and plan-splaining.

  • Promise: A shared impact map and development‑ready plan that removes ambiguity and speeds sign‑off.

Benefits

  • Aligned scope — Everyone sees the same blast radius.

  • Faster approvals — Plans with ACs and definition of done reduce debate.

  • Cleaner handoffs — Dev‑ready tickets with context avoid rework.

  • Variance protection — Coming soon: alerts on scope drift vs the approved requirement.

What you can do

  • Publish a requirement and auto‑generate its impact analysis.

  • Produce an implementation plan with ACs, estimates, and risks.

  • Sync to Jira and share to Slack for review.

  • Triage Variance alerts when implementation strays from scope (coming soon).
