Stay true to the plan

Variance compares commits and PRs to the approved requirement and plan—flagging discrepancies fast in Slack, Jira, and GitHub.

What it does

  • Compares commits/PRs vs requirement, impact map, tasks, and acceptance criteria.

  • Detects drift and posts actionable alerts to Slack/Jira.

  • Auto‑creates follow‑up Jira tickets for remediation.

  • Generates periodic variance reports with thresholds and ignore rules.

  • Protects velocity with fewer mid‑sprint surprises.

  • Improves quality by enforcing AC adherence.

  • Increases accountability with a clear plan‑vs‑build trail.

FAQs

  • What does Variance actually check? Drift like files changed outside the impact map, ACs not met, or tasks out of sequence.

  • Will this spam us with alerts? Sensitivity, ignore rules, and periodic rollups reduce noise.

  • What’s required to enable it? Connect GitHub, select repos, pick alert channels (Slack/Jira/Email).
